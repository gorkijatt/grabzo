Support

Write to grabzo.app@gmail.com. To help us find a problem, attach the app's log: Settings › About › Export Log.

A download is slow

Speed depends on the server. Many servers allow several connections per file; Grabzo uses up to 8 (up to 32 with Pro). Some servers allow only one. Keeping the app open gives full speed; when you leave it, iOS continues downloads more slowly in the background.

A download stopped or failed

Tap ↻ on the download to try again. It continues where it stopped when the server allows it. A link that has expired needs a fresh link: hold the download › Refresh Link.

A video can't be saved

The app doesn't save from media platforms such as YouTube or Instagram, and it can't save protected (DRM) videos.

Restore Pro

Settings › Grabzo Pro › Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

Cancel a subscription

iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions, or Settings › Grabzo Pro › Manage Subscription in the app.
