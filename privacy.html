Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

Grabzo is a download manager. It has no account and no analytics of its own. This page says what data the app handles, where it stays, and what the ads in the free version collect.

What stays on your device

Your downloads, files, browser history, favorites, tabs, cookies, statistics and settings are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. We never receive them. They are removed when you delete the app. Files you put in the Locker stay in the app and open only with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

Ads in the free version (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • your IP address, which gives a rough location;
  • which ads you see and tap;
  • crash and performance data of the ads software.

Google treats this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. In the EU, the UK and Switzerland you choose what Google may do in Google's consent form, and you can change it any time in the app under Settings › Privacy & Security › Ad Privacy Choices. You can also turn tracking off for every app in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Grabzo Pro has no ads, so none of this applies once you have Pro.

Purchases

Grabzo Pro is bought through Apple. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details; Apple only tells the app whether Pro is active.

Wi-Fi Sharing

When you turn Wi-Fi Sharing on, the app serves your files to devices on your own network, only while it is on, and only after the code shown on your phone is entered. Nothing goes over the internet.

Downloads and the browser

The app connects to the servers of the links you open or download, as any browser does. Those websites receive your IP address and the requests your device makes, under their own privacy policies. The app's Block Ads setting stops many advertising and tracking servers from loading in the browser.

Children

Grabzo is not directed at children and is rated 16+ on the App Store because of its web browser.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored on your device, deleting the app deletes your data. For data Google holds about ads, use Ad Privacy Choices in the app or Google's own tools linked above.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date. Questions: grabzo.app@gmail.com.
